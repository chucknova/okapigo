# The demo's photos: credits and licences

Ìrìn Lagos Tours is a fictional business. Its photos are real, openly licensed photos from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), each chosen and approved one by one
(2026-11-23). The files here are Commons' own 1280 px copies; the app makes resized copies of
them and changes nothing else. The app shows each photo's credit with it
("Photo: Sho photography, CC BY-SA 4.0").

The source of truth is [credits.js](credits.js); a test checks this page matches it.

The logo (`irin-logo.svg`, rendered to `irin-logo.png`) was drawn for the demo.
